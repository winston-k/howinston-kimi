# howinston-kimi

A complete mirror of [howinston.com](http://www.howinston.com), the homepage of
**Winston W. L. Ho** (Winston Ho Wing Long), preserved as a static site.

Mirrored on 2026-10-20 from the live site.

## Contents

- `index.html` : homepage - research profile, publications, awards, service
- `CV.pdf` : curriculum vitae
- `rep_PhD_thesis.pdf` , `rep_FYP_thesis.pdf` : NUS doctoral and honours theses
- `Pubs/` : 11 journal paper PDFs
- `pres_*.pdf` : 8 conference slide decks
- `CCF_Aaron_Rock_Jar_Hymnal.pdf` , `CCF_SSSS_Red_Book.pdf` , `CCF_SSSS_Yellow_Book.pdf` : Chinese Christian songbooks
- `Chinese_English_Dictionary.pdf` : searchable Chinese-English dictionary with hanyu pinyin
- `Jesus_Timeline.pdf` , `Optimism_by_Helen_Keller.pdf`
- `Geocities.html` + `Geocities/` : Yahoo Geocities archive pages
- `forum-letters-1.html` , `forum-letters-2.html`
- `who_photos.html` + `me001`-`me018` GIFs : photo page
- `research-links-technion.html` , `G_Home.html` , `who_contact.html`
- `EmailCleanUp1_0.zip` : EmailCleanUp 1.0 utility
